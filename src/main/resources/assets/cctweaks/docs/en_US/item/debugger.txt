Tired of testing broken scripts? Need some help? The all new
${**}Debug Wand${**} will help you on the way.

Shift right-clicking a running computer injects the Cobalt debug library
into its script, which unlocks getlocal, setlocal, getupvalue, setupvalue,
sethook and getinfo for debugging on the fly. It does not need to be
replaced after use.

***

On any other block it prints the block, its tile entity and the peripheral
attached to the side you clicked, along with the computers that peripheral
is bound to.

The wand only reaches computers within normal interaction range, and needs
the computer to be switched on when you click it.
